# Entitlement and plan rules

| Capability | Free account | Jfit Train | Jfit Pro |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Public catalogue browsing | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Entitled on-demand Jfit training | No | One user | Up to five separately authenticated seats |
| Programmes and personal progress | No | Yes | Yes, private per person |
| Owner billing access | N/A | Owner only | Owner only |
| Equipment screen/hardware control/partner services | No | Disabled | Disabled |

Launch price configuration: Train £14/month or £144/year; Pro £34/month or £349/year. The seed rows are inactive until tax, merchant and provider configuration is approved. No free trial or promotional price is configured. A verified provider event creates paid access; browser redirects are not evidence of payment. Cancellation preserves access through verified paid-through date; failed payment, refunds, grace periods and source-specific billing management need provider reconciliation before activation.

Pro includes owner plus at most four invited members. Active pending invitations reserve capacity. Invitation acceptance never silently cancels a member's existing paid plan. Removing a member preserves their account and private records, while shared entitlement ends according to the owner's paid-through period and configured seat policy.
